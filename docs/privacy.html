LensLoop Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

LensLoop is built to work almost entirely on your device. This policy explains exactly what data exists, where it lives, and who sees it.

What we store, and where

Lens, accessory, comfort-log, and prescription data you enter is stored locally on your device (and shared between the LensLoop app and its widgets via an iOS App Group container). This data is not uploaded to any server we operate. We do not have a backend, and we cannot see your lens data.

Prescription photos, if you add one, are stored the same way — locally on your device only.

Notifications are scheduled locally on your device to remind you about lens and accessory replacement. No push notification server is involved and no reminder content is transmitted anywhere.

Purchases

LensLoop offers weekly and yearly auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time "lifetime unlock" purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple and by our payment infrastructure provider, RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives a device/app-scoped identifier and purchase/transaction data necessary to validate your purchase and keep it unlocked across devices. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details. We do not receive or store your payment details — Apple handles all billing.

Anonymous usage counters

Starting with version 1.2.4, LensLoop counts a few steps on the way from installing the app to a purchase — for example that setup was finished, that notification permission was allowed or declined, that the plans screen was shown and where it was opened from, that a plan was tapped or bought, and how many times a pair was replaced. These are plain numbers and one short label (such as onboarding or settings), stored with RevenueCat alongside the anonymous app-scoped identifier described above. They are not linked to your name, email or any account, are not used for advertising or tracking, and are used only to understand which part of the app needs improving.

No lens, accessory, comfort-log, prescription or eye data is ever included in these counters — that data never leaves your device.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no ad tracking.
  • No account creation, no email collection.
  • No selling or sharing of your personal data with third parties for marketing.
  • No analytics SDKs — the only usage data is the anonymous counters described above, sent through RevenueCat.

Your control

Because your lens, accessory, and comfort data lives on your device, deleting the app deletes that data. You can remove specific entries directly in the app at any time.
