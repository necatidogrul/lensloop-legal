What LensLoop is

LensLoop is a personal reminder and logging tool for contact lens wear, lens accessories, and self-reported eye comfort. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Reminders are based on the schedule you enter — always follow your eye care professional's guidance over any reminder from this app. If you experience eye pain, redness, vision changes, or any symptom of concern, stop wearing your lenses and contact an eye care professional promptly.
